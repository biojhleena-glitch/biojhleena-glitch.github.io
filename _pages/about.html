About me

A materials engineer.
An RNA delivery researcher.

I’m a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Connecticut, working with Professor Yupeng Chen. I develop DNA-inspired Janus base nanomaterials to help therapeutic cargo travel through tissues and reach its site of action inside cells.

I earned my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering at UConn, my M.S. in Bioengineering at UC San Diego, and my B.S. in Convergence Biotechnology at Konkuk University. Before my doctoral training, I worked on biosensors at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology.

My current research brings RNA delivery together with engineered tissue models for osteoarthritis. I was selected for the 2026–2027 ORS/Bethel Fellowship, beginning in October 2026. Alongside research, I have mentored seven graduate and undergraduate researchers in nanomaterial synthesis, characterization, and RNA delivery.
