About me

From molecular design
to biological function.

I’m a postdoctoral researcher in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Connecticut. I study how the design of a nanomaterial shapes its journey through tissues and into cells.

My work brings together DNA-inspired chemistry, RNA delivery, and engineered tissue models to address barriers in cancer and osteoarthritis treatment.
